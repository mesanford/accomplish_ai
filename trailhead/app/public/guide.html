What changed in 2026

  • More than 80 badges received requirement updates effective January 1, 2026. Scouts who started before then may finish on the old requirements or switch.
  • Citizenship in Society was discontinued February 27, 2026. Eagle now has 13 required badges and 8 electives. Scouts who had started it may finish and count it for Eagle through December 31, 2026.
  • Indian Lore was renamed and rewritten as American Indian Culture in January 2026.

Eagle choice groups

Three Eagle slots let you pick one badge from a group. Cards marked with a group name count toward that slot:

  • Emergency Preparedness or Lifesaving
  • Environmental Science or Sustainability
  • Swimming or Hiking or Cycling

Test Lab badges

Scouting America's Test Lab pilots possible new badges, including Auctioneering, Life Skills, Physics, Sewing and Needlework, Dance, and Military Service & Veterans. They are not official merit badges yet and are not listed above.

How to read this guide

Summaries condense the official requirements and are no substitute for them. Difficulty is an editorial estimate based on required time spans, physical demands, special access (boats, ranges, horses) and the number of projects. Always work from the current pamphlet and the official requirements on scouting.org, and confirm with your merit badge counselor.
